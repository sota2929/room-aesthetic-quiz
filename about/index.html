How we make room recommendations

This is a small editorial project for renters, dorm residents, and first apartments. The goal is to help people measure, compare, and choose—not to make every product look essential.

Who creates this site

Room Aesthetic Quiz is an independent editorial project. Content, tools, and tests are maintained by the project owner under the Room Aesthetic Quiz name. We do not claim architectural, engineering, or interior-design licensure.

Advice is limited to general room-planning education. A real room can differ because of doors, closets, radiators, outlets, building rules, and exact furniture construction.

02 · The decision

How a product category earns a recommendation

  1. It solves a named constraint. Workspace, storage, lighting, or circulation comes before decoration.
  2. Its fit condition can be explained. Depth, width, clearance, mounting, or access must be stated.
  3. A compromise is visible. One-sided bed access or a smaller desk is not hidden.
  4. The reader can verify it. Current listing dimensions, seller, price, and return terms remain on Amazon.
03 · Visual evidence

How images and AI are used

Room photographs and some visual concepts may be created or refined with image-generation tools. They illustrate a style or planning idea; they are not photographs of the exact Amazon products linked on the page.

Measured diagrams, comparison tables, copy, fit rules, and product-fit conditions are edited for this project. Visual inspiration never overrides finished dimensions.

04 · Money

Affiliate and product independence

As an Amazon Associate, the project may earn from qualifying purchases. The price to the reader does not increase because of the link. There are currently no paid product placements or sponsored rankings.

The digital planning kit is sold by this project through Gumroad. Free guides and the fit checker remain available whether or not anyone buys it.

05 · Maintenance

Updates and corrections

Pages show an update date where practical. Product links may change because Amazon listings change. Fixed prices, review counts, and availability are not copied into articles.

If a recommendation cannot be supported by its dimensions or a page becomes misleading, the preferred fix is to correct or remove it—not to preserve the affiliate click.
