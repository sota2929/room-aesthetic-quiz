The quick answer

A standard full mattress is 54 × 75 inches inside a room measuring 96 × 120 inches. Use a frame close to the mattress footprint, a desk no deeper than 20 inches, and one continuous route at least 24 inches wide where possible.

Best default: Layout A. It creates the cleanest entry-to-closet route. If you need a monitor or long study sessions, Layout B gives the desk more breathing room.

Honest limit

If you need a full bed, a desk wider than 40 inches, and a dresser, an 8×10 room usually cannot keep all three functional. Replace the dresser with closet organizers—or choose a twin bed.

Common questions

8×10 bedroom FAQ

Can a full bed and desk fit?

Yes. Use a compact frame and a 30–36-inch desk. Keeping one long side of the bed against a wall creates the most usable route.

Is 24 inches enough for a walkway?

It is a tight-room compromise, not a comfort target. Aim closer to 30 inches on the main route where the room allows, and never obstruct required egress.

Where does clothing storage go?

Use the closet first, then under-bed space and the back of the door. Adding a dresser often forces the desk or full bed out of this room size.
