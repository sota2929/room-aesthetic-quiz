    How the math works

    Simple enough to use. Honest about its limits.

    01

    Two orientations

    The tool tests the room with the bed aligned in both directions and keeps the stronger simplified clearance.

    02

    24″ and 30″ signals

    Thirty inches is treated as a stronger circulation target. Twenty-four inches is labeled tight—not ideal.

    03

    No false precision

    Doors, closets, frames, radiators, and chair movement can invalidate the result. The verdict always asks you to measure them.
